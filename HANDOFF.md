The organization should own or control:

- domain registration
- GitHub repository
- Cloudflare account/site
- business email
- DNS configuration
- website source files
- logo
- business photographs

## 3. NEVER WRITE THESE HERE

Do not record in this document:

- passwords
- API keys
- authentication tokens
- recovery codes
- banking information
- sensitive personal information

## 4. SIMPLE WEBSITE CHANGES

Explain in plain language how the owner should request or make changes to:

- phone number
- email
- service area
- business description
- truck/equipment information
- photographs

Simply let Digital Foundation know what needs to be updated and they'll make the changes for you.

## 5. DOMAIN RENEWAL

Explain that the domain must remain renewed and that the organization should
maintain control of the registrar account.

The domain registration must be renewed before it expires. Keep the registrar
account active and in the organization's control to prevent service disruption.

## 6. ADMINISTRATOR CHANGES

Include a reminder to remove access for people who no longer need it and
maintain at least one organization-controlled administrator.

Remember to regularly review who has access to your digital assets. Remove
anyone who no longer needs access and ensure you always have at least one
administrator who is controlled by the organization.

## 7. IF DIGITAL FOUNDATION SERVICE ENDS

State clearly:

The organization retains ownership/control of its domain, website source,
GitHub repository, Cloudflare deployment, email configuration, and business
assets.

The website must remain portable to another provider.

If Digital Foundation stops providing services, you still own and control
all your digital assets. You can easily move your website and email to
another provider without losing access to your content.
