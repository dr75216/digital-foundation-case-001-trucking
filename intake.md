# Digital Foundation Case 001: Field Intake

This is a simple conversational intake that a nontechnical person can conduct with a very small business owner by phone, in person, or from a mobile device.

**Case 001:** One-person trucking company in Dallas, Texas.

## A. Business identity

**REQUIRED**
- Business name: __________
- Owner/contact name: __________
- Business phone: __________
- Business email (if one exists): __________
- City/state: __________

**OPTIONAL**
- Years in business: __________

## B. Trucking operation

**REQUIRED**
- Type of authority: [ ] Own authority [ ] Leased to carrier [ ] Other [ ] Unsure
- Tractor/equipment: __________
- Trailers used: __________
- Freight normally hauled: __________
- Normal operating/service area: __________
- Preferred lanes: __________

## C. How work is obtained

**REQUIRED**
Where do your loads/work usually come from? Check all that apply.
[ ] Load boards
[ ] Freight brokers
[ ] Dispatch service
[ ] Other carriers
[ ] Direct shippers
[ ] Referrals
[ ] Repeat customers
[ ] Other: __________

- Who usually finds the next load? __________
- About how many hours per week do you spend finding loads or handling brokers/dispatch? __________
- What would you most like to improve about how you get work? __________

## D. Digital presence

[ ] Website
[ ] Domain name: __________
[ ] Professional business email
[ ] Google Business Profile
[ ] Facebook
[ ] Other social media
[ ] Logo
[ ] Good photos of truck/equipment
[ ] None of these

## E. Credibility

**OPTIONAL - HIGH VALUE**

- What should a broker or potential customer know about your business? __________
- Why do brokers or customers call you again? __________
- When someone asks about your company today, what do you normally send or tell them? __________

Use the owner's own words. Do not rewrite answers during the interview.

## F. Goals

**REQUIRED**

If the business were working better six months from now, what would be different?

Which matter most? Choose up to 3.
[ ] More direct customers
[ ] More repeat lanes
[ ] Better-paying loads
[ ] Less deadhead
[ ] Less time searching for loads
[ ] More home time
[ ] More consistent work
[ ] Add another truck
[ ] Make the current truck more profitable
[ ] Other: __________

What would you like someone to do after finding your business online?
[ ] Call me
[ ] Email me
[ ] Ask about availability
[ ] Request a quote
[ ] Save/contact us as a potential carrier
[ ] Other: __________
